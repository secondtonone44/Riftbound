How each run works

Explore, upgrade,
keep moving.

Explore lower districts for more rewards, or climb early to stay ahead of the water. Your route decides which upgrades and enemies you meet.

  • 01 Choose a route through connected districts
  • 02 Combine weapons, items, and upgrades
  • 03 Reach the summit and defeat the boss

A run in three steps

01

Fight

Clear enemy waves and protect the route ahead.

02

Build

Choose weapons, items, and upgrades that work together.

03

Climb

Reach higher districts before the flood closes the lower map.
