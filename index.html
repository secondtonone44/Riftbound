Every character starts with a different weapon and ability. Pick the one that suits you, then change the build with upgrades found during the run.

GLASS HERETIC · RANGED

Redirect energy beams.

Use the crystal weapon to change the beam's path and hit several groups of enemies with one attack.

SIEGE SEXTON · HEAVY WEAPON

How a run works

LOOT.
UPGRADE.
KEEP MOVING.

Explore lower districts for more rewards, or climb early to stay ahead of the water. Your route decides which upgrades and enemies you meet.

  • 01 Choose a route through connected districts
  • 02 Combine weapons, items, and upgrades
  • 03 Reach the summit and defeat the boss
